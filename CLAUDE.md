## Project Context

The Chord twin of the Inform 7 practice game on the hub. Six rooms around an entrance hall, four discoveries worth ten points (the journal's clue, the coin, the ring under the stones, the locket in the locked cabinet), three ranks, and the same help system, USE handler, and synonym table the Inform edition uses to teach a first-time player. It exists so the two engines can be compared on identical content.

## Key Design Decisions (do not change without discussion)

- The journal is `openable, readable` so that OPEN JOURNAL commits and its `after` clause can award the points; the Inform edition intercepted opening with an Instead rule. READ JOURNAL prints the same passage.
- The flat stones are `pushable` scenery with `settled`/`shifted` states; the golden ring starts out of play and is moved into the Pond by the `after the player pushing` clause.
- Winning is a header daemon: `on every turn while` all four discoveries are in their done states, `win victory`. Inform checked the score every turn; Chord has no score condition, so each discovery carries a state.
- Direction synonyms (`head north`, `proceed up`) are `extend action going` patterns with `means direction <word>` lines. This is the only way to add movement phrasings.
- `use X on Y` is a hint, not an unlock. Routing it into the standard `unlocking` binds the first noun as the target, so the key ends up being the thing unlocked.
- The Inform edition's custom parser-error messages have no Chord equivalent; the platform's replies stand.
