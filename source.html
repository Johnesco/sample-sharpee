 419    This is a text adventure. Type commands in plain English.{br}
 420    Most puzzles can be solved with simple two-word commands: VERB NOUN.{br}
 421    Type VERBS for a full list of available commands.{br}
 422    Type LOOK to see your surroundings. Type INVENTORY (or I) to check what
 423    you carry.
 424  end phrase
